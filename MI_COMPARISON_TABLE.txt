INSIGHTS FOR MODEL IMPROVEMENT:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

To match or exceed cNVAE structure:

1. Add Hierarchical Constraints
   - Penalize MI between latents in different scales
   - Encourage scale-specific factor encoding
   
2. Improve Disentanglement
   - Implement β-TCVAE for total correlation minimization
   - Use Factor-VAE loss to align latents with factors
   - Add mutual information bottleneck constraints

3. Optimize Latent Utilization
   - Add sparsity-inducing regularization
   - Implement selective attention to active latents
   - Use pruning to remove underutilized dimensions

4. Scale-Specific Analysis
   - Compute per-scale MI heatmaps
   - Visualize factor distribution across scales
   - Enforce scale-factor alignment
